Welcome to The Greens

The stage is set for the world's largest pharmaceuticals industrial park. Opportunity awaits. Rain or shine, The Greens is the area's finest — make it your destination for a peaceful abode.

Touted to be the world's largest pharmaceuticals industrial park, Hyderabad Pharma City is set to be a one-stop solution for pharmaceutical companies in their manufacturing and development needs — one of the most modern industrial zones in the country, with every facility for investors to set up their manufacturing units.

Spread across 19,333 acres and expected to create around 420,000 jobs, the Pharma City will drive immense demand for housing in the nearby areas.

The Greens is just a short drive from all this activity, and yet feels like a world away. It offers peaceful life near the lively city of Hyderabad without compromising on greenery and seclusion. Its strategic location allows easy access to road networks that put many major employment centres within easy reach.
