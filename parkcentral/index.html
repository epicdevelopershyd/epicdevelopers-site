The northern growth corridor region of Hyderabad and the most in-demand emerging location, Dundigal, is a quick drive from world-class schools, educational institutions and employment centres. Its transport infrastructure is excellent too, with superb connectivity to the rest of Hyderabad via the Outer Ring Road and regular buses linking the area to other parts of the city. With the proposed IT Park coming up in 300+ acres in Dundigal, along with a proposal from the government to provide incentives to companies that set up in this area, Dundigal is all set for explosive growth.

Set in the tranquility of Dundigal, away from all this hustle-and-bustle, is Park Central — a quiet, private and beautiful premium plotted development from Epic Developers, offering an exceptional array of home sites to choose from. Surrounded by first-class amenities, this exclusive development showcases pristine landscaping and is dotted with tree-lined avenues.

Well-planned internal street network, walking and jogging trails, robust storm, water, electrical and sanitary systems — you can rest assured about the high standards of development at Park Central. The place to build your dream home. The place to build upon your legacy.
