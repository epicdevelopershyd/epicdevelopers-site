Eden Gardens

Sitting majestically on the Gandipet–Shankarpally main road, Eden Gardens marked a defining moment in Mokila's urban transformation. This guard-gated community boasts thoughtfully designed spaces, a clubhouse, walking and jogging trails, proximity to major employment centres, and gorgeous neighbourhood communities.

A magnificent community

The peaceful, clean and green living environment of Eden Gardens helps residents relax, rejuvenate and de-stress completely. These HMDA-approved premium villa plots are located near established neighbourhoods and in close proximity to a wide range of amenities — banks, schools, colleges, hospitals, eateries, supermarkets, shopping centres, urban employment centres, transportation corridors and leisure attractions.

An enchanting clubhouse

A modern private clubhouse sits at the heart of Eden Gardens. Whether it's savouring a cup of coffee against the plush landscape, small talk with residents after a morning walk, kids laughing on the playground, special occasions at the grand multipurpose hall, or an exhilarating workout in the state-of-the-art gym overlooking the greenery — it's all here.

Upgraded sector road

The Gandipet to Shankarpalli road, stretching over 20 kilometres, has been upgraded at a cost of around ₹100 Cr into an ultra-modern, four-lane signal-free expressway — drastically cutting travel time from Gachibowli, Shankarpalli and surrounding areas. The railway underpass between Shankarpalli and Mokila has further eased traffic flow.
