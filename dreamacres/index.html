Our lives can move too fast. We all have a desire to get away from the chaos of everyday life, to unwind after an active week, to get away from it all. The occasional change of scenery is not only desired but also much needed. That's exactly what Dream Acres is all about.

These half-acre farm properties provided the ultimate platform for those wishing to have their own farm houses with ample space, light and air, in plush landscaped environs. Nestled in the quiet stillness of Dasarlapally on the Hyderabad–Srisailam highway, Dream Acres effortlessly blends the best of rural charm with serene landscaped beauty.

Like a timeless work of art, farm properties are often prestigious trophies and legacy investments passed down through generations. For the families of Dream Acres, that legacy has begun.
